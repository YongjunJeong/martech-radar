# Data and demo notes

The offline demo uses fictional companies and generated observations. It does
not visit websites or read an existing workspace. Companies are marked `[DEMO]`;
the bundled screenshot shows only these synthetic records.

Vendor names and domains identify supported technologies from an independent,
third-party perspective. Their inclusion does not imply affiliation,
endorsement or a customer relationship.

Watchlists, scan databases, evidence, logs, backups and deployment settings stay
local and are excluded from Git. Real scans can contain account identifiers,
URLs and free-text evidence; use synthetic records for public screenshots.

Review staged changes before publishing exports. `.gitignore` covers common
local secrets and generated files, but cannot protect force-added files or
arbitrarily renamed exports.
